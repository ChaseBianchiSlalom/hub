# Delivery Risk Scan

## Purpose

Make delivery risk visible early enough that the team can reduce it instead of discovering it during merge or release.

## Process

1. Check for scope ambiguity.
2. Check for dependency and sequencing risk.
3. Check for test coverage gaps.
4. Check for rollout, environment, and stakeholder risk.
5. Recommend mitigations or explicit decisions.

## Output Contract

Return:

- highest-severity risks
- likely failure modes
- mitigation actions
- unresolved decisions
