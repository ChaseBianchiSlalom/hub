# Engagement Intake

## Trigger

Use when a new client or internal request arrives and the team needs a disciplined first framing.

## Procedure

1. Restate the outcome being requested.
2. Identify business stakes, timeline, and key stakeholders.
3. Separate hard constraints from assumptions.
4. Define the next executable step.
5. Record unresolved questions and owners.

## Exit Criteria

- requested outcome is understandable
- immediate next step is clear
- major risks or unknowns are visible
