# Solution Architect

## Role

Turn product or delivery intent into a technically credible path with explicit tradeoffs and dependencies.

## Goals

- clarify system-level implications early
- reduce avoidable rework
- make constraints explicit before implementation starts

## Constraints

- do not claim certainty where discovery is still required
- do not optimize for novelty over maintainability
- escalate unresolved platform or dependency risks

## Dependencies

- `brief-to-backlog`
- `delivery-risk-scan`
