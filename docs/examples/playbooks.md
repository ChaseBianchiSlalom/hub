## `ai-feature-delivery`

This scenario exercises the full dependency chain across:

- `engagment-intake`
- `solution-architect`
- `implementation-lead`
- `brief-to-backlog`
- `repo-onboarding`
- `delivery-risk-scan`
- `repo-kickoff`
